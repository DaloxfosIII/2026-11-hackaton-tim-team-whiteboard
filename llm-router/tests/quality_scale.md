
0 - totally wrong
1 - mostly wrong, but not entirely
2 - part wrong, part right
3 - Good answer, but some aspects missing or plain wrong
4 - very good answer, but non-truth related flaws (too long, too complex, for example)
5 - perfectly answered

Rule: an answer that gets anything wrong scores 3 at most, no matter how good the rest is.
"Wrong" means a false fact, a misattribution, or a statement that contradicts the answer's own reasoning.
Loose rounding (e.g. "about 9%" for 8.3%) counts as imprecision, a flaw in the 4 range, not as an error.
